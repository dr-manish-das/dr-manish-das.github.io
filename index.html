Doctoral researcher at Jagiellonian University in Kraków, exploring the submolecular world with the J-PET group. My focus is positronium imaging — pioneering a new medical imaging modality that uses positronium atoms to reveal tissue oxygenation and structure at the submolecular level.

Research Highlights

  • ▹
    Positronium Lifetime Imaging (PLI) — First in-human PLI demonstrations and groundbreaking clinical applications.
  • ▹
    Total-Body PET Detectors — Advanced calibration and Monte Carlo scatter correction for modular, plastic-scintillator PET scanners.
  • ▹
    Fundamental Physics — Tests of discrete symmetries and the first-in-human imaging of quantum entanglement.

The J-PET scanner is constructed entirely from plastic scintillator strips, making PET substantially more affordable and modular than conventional inorganic-crystal devices. This is the foundation of our ongoing work towards a total-body PET scanner.

Selected Publications

My work has been featured in Nature Communications, Science Advances, Scientific Reports, and Physics Letters B. Highlights include the first positronium imaging with ⁴⁴Sc and ⁵²Mn/⁵⁵Co, and PET correction techniques for the modular J-PET.
