# Manish Das

**Doctoral Researcher · Experimental Medical Physics**
_Positronium lifetime imaging with the J-PET plastic-scintillator PET scanner_

I am a PhD researcher at the Marian Smoluchowski Institute of Physics, Jagiellonian University, working in the
[J-PET group](https://koza.if.uj.edu.pl/pet) led by Prof. Paweł Moskal. My thesis is on positronium lifetime imaging with a
PET detector built entirely from plastic scintillator strips, covering radionuclide selection, TOF/TOT calibration,
scatter and random correction, and image reconstruction for triple-coincidence data. I also contribute to tests of
fundamental P, T and CP symmetries using annihilation photons from positronium.

## Research

When a positron meets an electron, the two can briefly bind into an atom of matter and antimatter: **positronium**. In
tissue, the time between its formation and its annihilation depends on the surrounding molecular environment and on
oxygen concentration, so measuring that lifetime yields a functional map at submillimetre scale that a conventional PET
scan cannot resolve. **Positronium lifetime imaging (PLI)** turns this into an image — for example of tumour
oxygenation. Because the J-PET scanner is assembled entirely from plastic scintillator strips, PET built this way is
cheaper, lighter and more modular than conventional inorganic-crystal detectors, and it is the starting point for
ongoing work towards a total-body plastic PET scanner.

Parallel to imaging, I work on fundamental-symmetry tests in positronium decays, using the same annihilation-photon
photons and the same detector as probes of P, T and CP violation.
