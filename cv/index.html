Professional Summary

Experimental physicist working on positronium imaging — a new PET modality that images the lifetime of positronium atoms inside tissue as a probe of submolecular structure and oxygenation. Part of the J-PET research group at the Jagiellonian University in Kraków, where the plastic-scintillator J-PET scanners are developed. Author of 25 papers on PET detector corrections, radionuclide selection for triple-coincidence imaging, positronium lifetime reconstruction and tests of fundamental symmetries with annihilation photons. First-author and equal-contribution work covers positronium lifetime imaging with the novel ⁴⁴Sc, ⁵²Mn and ⁵⁵Co radionuclides, Monte Carlo scatter correction for the modular J-PET, and the first experimental PLI demonstration with ⁴⁴Sc.
