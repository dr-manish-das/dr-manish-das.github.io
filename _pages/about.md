## About me

I am a doctoral researcher at the **Jagiellonian University** in Kraków, working in the
[J-PET](https://jpet.if.uj.edu.pl/) group of the Department of Experimental Particle Physics and
Applications. My research focuses on **positronium imaging** — a new medical imaging modality that
uses the lifetime of positronium atoms as a probe of the submolecular structure and oxygenation of
living tissue.

Positronium is formed abundantly in the molecular voids of the body during PET. Conventional PET
scanners record only the two 511 keV annihilation photons and therefore say nothing about how long
the atom lived before annihilating. By also registering the *prompt gamma* emitted alongside the
positron, the J-PET detectors recover the time of positronium formation and turn that lifetime
into a **new dimension of contrast** — complementary to the anatomical, morphological and metabolic
images that PET already provides.

### Research

- **Positronium lifetime imaging (PLI)** — the first in-human images with ⁶⁸Ga and with the
  brain of a glioblastoma patient, and the first PLI demonstrations with the ⁴⁴Sc, ⁵²Mn and ⁵⁵Co
  radionuclides.
- **Plastic-scintillator PET detectors** — TOF and TOT calibration of the modular J-PET, quality
  control of scintillator strips for the total-body scanner, and Monte Carlo scatter correction for
  quantitative reconstruction.
- **Fundamental physics with annihilation photons** — tests of P, T and CP invariance in
  ortho-positronium decay and the first-in-human imaging of quantum entanglement between
  annihilation photons.

The J-PET scanner is built entirely from plastic scintillator strips, which makes PET markedly more
affordable and modular than conventional inorganic-crystal devices — the point of the ongoing work
towards a **total-body PET scanner** made of plastic.

### Selected publications

My work appears in *Nature Communications*, *Science Advances*, *Scientific Reports* and
*Physics Letters B*, among others. First-author and equal-contribution papers include the first
positronium imaging with ⁴⁴Sc and with ⁵²Mn/⁵⁵Co, the PET correction techniques for the modular
J-PET, and the first PLI demonstration with ⁴⁴Sc. The full list — 25 papers, conference
proceedings and posters — is on the [publications page](/publications/).

I am a member of the J-PET collaboration and the **muPPET** experiment. I have also spent a research
internship at the **University of California, Davis**.
